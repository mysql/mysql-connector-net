Connector/NET 9.7 Release Notes
------------------------------------
Welcome to the release notes for Connector/NET 9.7

What's new in 9.7
--------------------

Connector/NET now has improved performance for synchronous operations.
Connector/NET enhanced EF Core 10 compatibility by adding support for `StartsWith` and `EndsWith` in LINQ queries.
Connector/NET fixed an issue that could cause missing rows in specific data-retrieval scenarios.
